我的AI助理（My AI Assistant）

這是 WTC-tommy 自己開發、僅供本人使用的私人 AI 助理，不對外提供服務，也不開放其他人使用。

This is a private AI assistant built and used only by its owner (GitHub: WTC-tommy). It is not offered to anyone else.

隱私權政策（Privacy Policy）

  • 本程式只存取擁有者本人授權的 Google 帳戶資料（例如 Google 雲端硬碟中的會議錄音、案件清單），用途是為擁有者整理會議紀錄與報告。
  • 不收集、不儲存其他任何人的 Google 帳戶資料。
  • 資料不會出售、不會用於廣告，也不會分享給第三方；僅在處理時傳送給執行該功能所需的服務（例如語音轉文字與 AI 模型服務）。
  • 擁有者可隨時在 Google 帳戶的「第三方存取權」中撤銷授權。

The app accesses only the owner's own Google account data that the owner has authorized (for example meeting recordings and case lists in Google Drive), solely to prepare meeting minutes and reports for the owner. It does not collect data from anyone else, does not sell or share data, and does not use it for advertising. Data is sent only to the services needed to perform the task (such as speech-to-text and AI model providers). The owner can revoke access at any time in their Google Account settings.
